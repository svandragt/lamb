- The `posts_per_page` setting. Lists show 10 posts per page. ([#920](https://github.com/svandragt/lamb/issues/920))
- The `404_fallback` setting. An unknown address now always shows the 404 page; add a `[redirections]` entry for each old address you still want to forward. ([#920](https://github.com/svandragt/lamb/issues/920))
- The `[preconnect]` setting. Lamb adds preconnect hints only for the sites a page's posts load images and video from. ([#920](https://github.com/svandragt/lamb/issues/920))
- The `micropub_debug` setting. Set the `LAMB_MICROPUB_DEBUG` environment variable to turn on Micropub diagnostic logging. ([#920](https://github.com/svandragt/lamb/issues/920))
- The `experimental_features` setting. Pass `--experimental` to `bin/lamb import` to run an experimental importer. ([#920](https://github.com/svandragt/lamb/issues/920))
- The placeholder author "Joe Sheeple". A site with no author name now shows an empty author in its feeds and page metadata. ([#920](https://github.com/svandragt/lamb/issues/920))
- A removed setting that is still in your configuration is ignored, and the site keeps working. Saving the settings reports a `[preconnect]` section that still holds entries as ignored. ([#920](https://github.com/svandragt/lamb/issues/920))
