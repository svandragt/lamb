- PHP 8.6 is now tested and supported, alongside 8.4 and 8.5. On PHP 8.6, RedBeanPHP 5.7.6 raises a deprecation notice on requests that touch the database. If `display_errors` is on, the notice appears in the page and can stop Lamb sending headers such as redirects and the login cookie, so turn `display_errors` off on 8.6. The notices go away when RedBeanPHP ships its fixes ([#853](https://github.com/svandragt/lamb/issues/853)).
